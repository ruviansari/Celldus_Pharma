2. Public Commercial Website Guide

The public website displays live dynamic data fetched directly from the PostgreSQL/SQLite database models without any static placeholders.

1 Interactive Homepage (/home/)
  • Live Product Portfolio Counter: Automatically counts total active formulations in the database (e.g. 8+) and displays animated metrics.
  • Dynamic Category Filter Tabs: Quick filter pills (Antibiotics, Pediatric, Gynaecology, etc.) that link directly to commercial catalog subsets.
  • Clinical Insights / Blogs: Latest 3 articles automatically rendered from the live database table.
2 Commercial Product Catalog (/product/)
  • Category Quick-Pills: Horizontal clickable badge pills (e.g., "All Categories", "Pediatric Drops", "Antibiotics"). Clicking any pill instantly filters formulations with zero page reload glitches.
  • Live Search Bar: Type active salt names (e.g. Paracetamol, Cefixime, Amoxicillin) to filter catalog formulations in real-time.
  • Inquire Action Button: Each product card features a direct "Inquire" button which automatically prefills the formulation name into the commercial inquiry form.
3 Formulation Technical Details (/product_details/<id>/)
  • Specifications: Pack sizes (e.g. 30 ml / 10x10 Tablets), dosage forms, indications, and WHO-GMP compliance badges.
  • Related Formulations: Bottom carousel automatically loads other live medicines belonging to the identical therapeutic category.
  • Safe Breadcrumb Navigation: Clickable breadcrumbs safely return users to /home/ without broken 404 relative links.
4 Customer Inquiries & Lead Generation (/contact/)
  • Visitors, hospital procurement officers, and stockists submit trade inquiries through this form.
  • Each submission is immediately recorded in the live database and generates an instant inquiry card in the staff dashboard.

3. Commercial Catalog Management (Admin)

Staff members can maintain the entire public product catalog through clean dashboard interfaces without writing a single line of code.

3.1 Therapeutic Categories (/dashboard/categories/)

How to Add a Category:
  1. Navigate to http://127.0.0.1:8000/dashboard/categories/.
  2. In the top form, enter the Category Name (e.g. "Dermatology & Skin Care") and an optional description.
  3. Click "Save Category".
  4. The category immediately appears on the public website (/product/) as a new filter pill and in the blog sidebars.

3.2 Medicines & Formulations (/dashboard/products/)

How to Publish a New Formulation to the Website:
  1. Navigate to http://127.0.0.1:8000/dashboard/products/.
  2. Click "+ Add New Product".
  3. Fill in the commercial details:
    • Product Name: Brand name + Strength (e.g. "CEFDUS-O 200 DT").
    • Therapeutic Category: Select from your live category dropdown.
    • Pack Size: Commercial packaging (e.g. "10x10 Tablets Strip").
    • Commercial Formulation Details / Description: Composition & clinical indications.
    • Product Packaging Image: Upload standard medicine box image.
  4. Click "Save Product".
  5. The formulation is now immediately live on the homepage, products catalog, and searchable by buyers.

3.3 Customer Trade Inquiries (/dashboard/customers/)

Every commercial inquiry submitted via the website appears in this unified customer CRM desk:

  • Clickable Inquiry Cards: Displays the buyer's full name, email, phone number, subject, timestamp, and requirements message.
  • Interactive Smooth Focus: Clicking any inquiry card cleanly highlights the conversation without any horizontal UI drift.
  • Newsletter Subscribers: Access the list of verified emails subscribed to pharmaceutical updates.
