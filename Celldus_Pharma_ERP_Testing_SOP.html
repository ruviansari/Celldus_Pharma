PHASE 1: QC TESTING & QA ELECTRONIC DISPOSITION (21 CFR PART 11)
STEP 1 Quality Dashboard & Interactive Filters Test /dashboard/quality/
  1. Browser me /dashboard/quality/ URL open karein.
  2. Top KPI Cards check karein: Pending Sampling (2), Under Analytical Testing (2), QA Dispositioned (1) show hone chahiye.
  3. Card Click Test: "Pending Sampling / Tests" card par click karein → Table automatically filter hokar sirf pending requests show karegi.
  4. "Under Analytical Testing" card par click karein → Sirf testing-in-progress batch show hoga.
  5. Table ke upar bane search box me Paracetamol ya B-SOL-2601 type karke filter test karein.
Expected Output: Sabhi cards smoothly clickable hain, active state me green/amber/cyan glow hota hai aur table instant filter hoti hai bina page crash ke.
STEP 2 21 CFR Part 11 Electronic Batch Release Modal Test /dashboard/quality/
  1. Kisi bhi pending request (jaise LOT-EXC-2026-001) ke samne "QA Action" button dabayein.
  2. Naya redesigned modal open hoga jisme Inspection No, Material, Batch, aur Lot card clearly dikhega.
  3. QA Decision: "AUTHORIZE RELEASE" chunein → System Certificate of Analysis (COA) number ko mandatory karega.
  4. COA No: Enter karein COA-2026-MCC-0104.
  5. Rationale: Regulatory reason likhein (min 5 chars) jaise: Pharmacopoeial monograph testing verified, complies with IP standard.
  6. "Sign & Authorize Release" button par click karein.
Expected Output: Batch successfully RELEASED ho jayega, stock QUARANTINE se AVAILABLE me move ho jayega, aur green success alert show hoga.
PHASE 2: MANUFACTURING & ELECTRONIC BMR BATCH PRODUCTION
STEP 3 Manufacturing Batches & Yield Reconciliation Test /dashboard/production/
  1. Browser me /dashboard/production/ open karein.
  2. KPI Cards check karein: Active Manufacturing Batches (2) aur Completed Batches (1).
  3. Clickable Cards: "Active Batches" card par click karein → Sirf B-CPT-2026-003 aur B-CPT-2026-004 show honge. "Completed" card click karne par sirf completed batch aayega.
  4. Batch Completion Action: In-Progress batch ke aage "Complete Batch" button par click karein.
  5. Modal me Actual Produced Qty (e.g. 4950) aur Scrap Qty (e.g. 50) enter karke "Submit to QC" dabayein.
Expected Output: Batch status "COMPLETED" ban jayega, Yield percentage automatically calculate hogi (99.00%), aur raw material inventory deduct hokar naya finished lot QC Quarantine me hand-off ho jayega.
